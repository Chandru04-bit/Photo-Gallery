Complete 2026 Wedding Infrastructure Checklist

A step-by-step master planner guide covering staging, pandal sizing, electrical load, and vendor coordination.

Architectural Precision & Cultural Sanctity

Planning an Indian wedding involves coordinating dozens of rituals, hundreds of guests, and complex spatial infrastructure. Between caterers, photographers, priests, and florists, having a structured timeline prevents last-minute stress.

This comprehensive guide walks you through the critical infrastructure milestones every family should verify from 6 months before the big day through the final muhurtham morning.

Master Architect Insight

"Every celebration deserves structural perfection. From the load-tested platform beneath your feet to the fragrant floral canopy overhead, excellence is in the details."

1. Six Months Out: Venue Recce & Date Locking

Book your staging and pandal contractor early, especially during peak muhurtham dates. Conduct an on-site technical recce to assess venue ceiling height, doorway clearances for large furniture, and ground leveling requirements for outdoor marquees.

2. Two Months Out: 3D Blueprints & Electrical Audits

Review finalized photorealistic 3D CAD blueprints of your stage, mandap, and entrance gates. Calculate total electrical load including stage lights, catering warmers, air cooling, and sound systems to ensure sufficient silent generator capacity.

3. Week of the Event: Material Staging & Early Handover

Ensure hardware loading begins 24 hours prior to the event. Insist on a strict handover protocol where stage lights, sound checks, and floor cleaning are completed 4 to 6 hours before muhurtham, allowing the family a peaceful, unhurried walkthrough.
