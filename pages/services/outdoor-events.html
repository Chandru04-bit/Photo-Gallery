WEATHERPROOF MARQUEE

Engineered German Marquee Tents & Sprawling Lawn Arenas

Hosting an outdoor event on a sprawling lawn, beachside resort, or temple ground demands reliable weather protection without sacrificing architectural beauty. UTSAV owns over 150,000 square feet of German clear-span aluminum marquee structures.

Our marquees have zero internal center poles, granting 100% unobstructed internal floor space for guest tables, dance floors, buffet lanes, and performance stages. Tested to withstand up to 100 km/h winds and tropical downpours.

Engineering & Artistry

Technical Specifications & Structural Craftsmanship

We combine traditional handicraft, safety-certified hardware, and high-CRI illumination for unmatched reliability.

Clear-Span German Aluminum Profiles

Fabricated from hard-pressed extruded aluminum alloy 6061/T6 with galvanized steel connectors. Covered with 850 GSM fire-retardant, UV-resistant, and blackout PVC coated fabric. Clear transparent glass-wall and roof options allow breathtaking starlight views.

  • Clear-span widths from 10 meters (33 ft) to 40 meters (131 ft) with unlimited length
  • Modular bay increments of 5 meters allowing expandable capacity from 200 to 5,000+ people
  • Optional crystal-clear PVC transparent roof panels for magical evening lawn views

Integrated Air Conditioning & Subflooring

We supply turnkey elevated timber flooring systems with steel sub-bases that compensate for uneven lawn topography. Ducting integrates with 10-ton to 50-ton packaged industrial air conditioning units to maintain a cool 21°C inside regardless of summer heat.

  • Heavy-duty tongue-and-groove wooden cassette flooring capable of supporting heavy cars
  • Industrial packaged AC units and quiet distribution ducting along marquee edges
  • Rain-sealed perimeter drainage trenches and entrance ramp walkways
