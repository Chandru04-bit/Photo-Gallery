Custom 3D Theme Backdrops & Cutouts

From whimsical Jungle Safari, Princess Castles, and Outer Space for children, to Hollywood Red Carpet, Retro Disco, and Rose Gold Champagne for adults. Our CNC cut-outs and hand-painted dimensional backdrops create immersive photo moments.

  • Set of 3 cylinder acrylic cake plinths with interior fairy lights or floral fill
  • Interactive photobooth station with matching hand-held props and instant backdrop
