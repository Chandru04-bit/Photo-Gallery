Who We Are

South India's Most Trusted Stage & Tent Contractor

UTSAV Stage, Tent & Pandal Contractors is a full-service event infrastructure company. We specialize in designing and constructing wedding stages, traditional marriage pandals, reception sets, and German marquee canopies for high-profile weddings, cultural festivals, and corporate gatherings.

With our own dedicated fleet of transport trucks, over 150,000 square feet of German aluminum tents, and an in-house carpentry and floral studio, we execute grand-scale celebrations with uncompromised precision, safety, and aesthetic beauty.

Our Heritage

Celebrating 18+ Years of Event Artistry

UTSAV began in 2008 with a simple yet ambitious vision: to elevate traditional Indian celebrations into regal, unforgettable visual spectacles without compromising structural safety or cultural authenticity.

Over the past two decades, what started as a family-run artisan workshop in Chennai has expanded into an industry-leading event infrastructure firm operating across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana. We have had the honor of decorating over 2,800 weddings, receptions, and public festivals.

From high-profile celebrity destination weddings to reverent temple festivals and multi-day corporate conferences, our master riggers and decor designers bring the same unyielding dedication to perfection.
