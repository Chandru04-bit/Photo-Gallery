Practical Advice

Essential Event Planning Tips

Avoid common decor pitfalls with these pro tips from seasoned riggers, florists, and lighting technicians.

Book 6 Months Early

Auspicious wedding dates (muhurtham days) book out fast. Block your stage and tent infrastructure 6 months ahead to secure choice materials.

Sync Lighting with Cameras

Always connect your decorator with your cinematographer. 95+ CRI warm wash lights ensure skin tones appear natural in 4K wedding films.

Plan for Rain Scenarios

Even during sunny seasons, sudden tropical showers can occur. Insist on German waterproof marquee tents with raised wooden subflooring.
